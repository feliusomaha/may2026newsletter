Applejack: A Brave Girl Facing Big Challenges
From the moment Applejack arrived at Felius, we knew she had a story. She came to us through our wonderful partners at Stray to Spay, and she'd barely had a chance to settle in before we noticed something was off. Applejack wasn't quite acting like herself. At Felius, we don't let things like that slide.
We got her in quickly to see Dr. Coleman at Completely Cat Clinic, where it became clear she was dehydrated and uncomfortable. Applejack was not thrilled about her blood draw or her x-ray (honestly, fair), but she held her head high through the whole thing. What a trooper.
Her results surprised us. X-rays revealed what appears to be an old fracture on her left femur. A window into a past we can only imagine. We consulted the specialists at Sirius Veterinary Orthopedic Center to help us formulate a plan. And as if that weren't enough, Applejack may also be facing FIP.
 
She's currently receiving supportive care here at Felius, and we are cautiously watching how she responds to treatment. She won’t be ready for adoption until we fully understand her prognosis.
Cats like Applejack's are exactly why we do this work. And why your support matters so deeply. Help us give Applejack (and cats like her) the care they deserve. Donate at felius.org/donate

Adoptable Cat Feature: Meet Hazel
 
 
 
Hazel is a stunning 4-year-old dilute tortie with the softest fur and an even softer heart to match. She can be a little shy at first, but give her a calm space and a kind hand, and she'll be rubbing against your legs before the week is out. This girl genuinely loves people and isn't afraid to show it.
She would thrive in a patient home that lets her settle in on her own terms — and rewards her handsomely with cuddles. She's the kind of cat who turns a house into a home.
